Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Overview

This application is a personal-use tool that interacts with Google APIs (Google Drive, Gmail, and Google Calendar) on behalf of the account owner. It is not distributed to the public and has fewer than 100 users.

Data Access

The application requests access to the following Google services via OAuth 2.0:

  • Google Drive: Read, write, and manage files and folders for file synchronization purposes.
  • Gmail: Read, send, and manage email messages for personal automation.
  • Google Calendar: Read, create, and manage calendar events for scheduling automation.

Data Usage

  • All data accessed through this application belongs to the account owner and is used solely for personal automation tasks.
  • No data is shared, sold, or transmitted to third parties.
  • No data is stored beyond what is necessary for the operation of the tools, and all data remains within the owner's Google account and local machines.

Data Retention

OAuth refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's machines to maintain continuous access. No user data is retained on external servers.
