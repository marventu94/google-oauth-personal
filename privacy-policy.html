Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Overview

These personal tools use Google Drive and Google Calendar on behalf of the account owner. They are not distributed to the public.

Data Access

The application requests access to the following Google services via OAuth 2.0:

  • Google Drive: rclone reads, writes, and manages files and folders to synchronize the owner's devices.
  • Google Calendar: LocalEventsToCal reads, creates, and updates events in the owner's calendars.

Data Usage

  • The tools use Google data only for the synchronization and calendar functions described above.
  • Data is not sold or shared with third parties.
  • Files remain in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's devices. Calendar events remain in the owner's Google Calendar.

Data Retention

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored on the owner's devices and backed up in the owner's Google Drive. The owner may remove local copies, backups, or the app's access at any time.

Contact

For questions, contact the owner via this project's GitHub issues.
